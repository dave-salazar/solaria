Solaria Parfums

Our story

Solaria was founded on a simple belief: a signature scent should not be a luxury reserved for a few.

We create fragrances inspired by the world’s most admired perfumes, and offer them at a price that lets you wear a different one for every mood, every day and every occasion.

Why we exist

Our emblem brings together a bottle, a sun and a compass: scent as light, and a guide to finding the fragrance that feels like you.

Our promise

Each Solaria fragrance is described honestly as inspired by its original. We are an independent brand and are not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the houses behind those originals.
